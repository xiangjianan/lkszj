### 【Basic Rules】

1. You may submit works of any form related to "creating beauty". This includes but is not limited to music, painting, photography, design, writing, physical works, and more.
2. The work must be completed independently or primarily by you and must be original; entries on behalf of others are not allowed (*On originality: pure covers, cover performances, and reproductions of existing works are not eligible for awards; remixes or material reworkings with your own creative ideas are eligible).
3. The work must have been completed after January 1, 2020 (inclusive), and must comply with national laws and policies. It must not contain dangerous, illegal, or sensitive content, and must not violate public order and good morals.
4. You must hold complete copyright over the submitted work.
5. Collective commercial works and award-winning works from other contests are not eligible for awards.
